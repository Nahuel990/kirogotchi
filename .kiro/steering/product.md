# Product steering

Kirogotchi is Schrödinger's Kiro: a ghost pet that is dead and alive until
you look, where every look is a single-shot measurement on Amazon Braket.

Rules for any change:

- Only `/look` may run a circuit. Reading the record is never a measurement.
- Every game mechanic must map to a real quantum operation (gate or channel)
  and the mapping must be stated in the UI. No fake quantum.
- Be honest in copy: the qubit is re-prepared from the record on each look.
- The pet is the Kiro ghost. Keep the trademark notice in README and footer.
- Tone: haunting, dry, short sentences. No exclamation marks in UI copy.
- No emojis anywhere.
